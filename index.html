What It Does

More than a logbook. A fishing intelligence engine.

Every catch you log becomes data. fishinglog.ai connects it all — tides, weather, moon, location, gear — and finds patterns you'd never spot alone.

🎙️

Smart Logging

Speak or type your catch. We auto-categorize species, bait, location, conditions, and gear. No fumbling with forms on the water.

🔬

Pattern Recognition

We connect every catch to tide state, weather fronts, moon phase, water temp, and GPS location — then surface the patterns that matter.

🎯

Predictive Insights

Based on your history, not generic advice. Get suggestions for where and when to fish tomorrow, this weekend, or next season.

🧠

Continuous Learning

How It Works

Dynamic Cognition Amplification

A local AI watches your logs and patterns continuously. A larger AI improves how it thinks about your fishing. Over time, it becomes a personal fishing guide that never stops learning.

1

Local Thinker

A lightweight AI lives on your device. It watches every log entry, tracks conditions in real-time, and builds your personal fishing model — all offline, all private.

2

The Conductor

A larger intelligence periodically reviews your patterns, refines the model, and sends improved strategies back to your device. Think of it as a master guide coaching your local expert.

3

Your Fishing Co-Pilot

The loop repeats. Every trip, every catch, every changing season makes the system smarter. It's not an app — it's a guide that grows with you.

Privacy First

Your fishing spots stay yours. Location data is processed locally and never shared. Your competitive edge is your data — we protect it.
